# Topical Authority — Genset.co.id

Last reviewed: 2026-07-23 (Asia/Jakarta)

## Role and boundary

Genset.co.id should become an Indonesian reference for generator sets across load definition, sizing, electrical and mechanical systems, fuel, installation, transfer and synchronization, commissioning, operation, maintenance, failure diagnosis, overhaul, procurement, emissions, and lifecycle decisions.

Primary readers include homeowners, facility teams, engineers, contractors, technicians, rental users, procurement teams, factories, hospitals, data centers, project sites, and businesses planning backup or prime power.

Cannibalization control applies only among URLs on Genset.co.id. Other owned domains may independently cover the same topic or query. Neutral education belongs under `/artikel/`; current product, rental, installation, service, maintenance, overhaul, enclosure, tank, and electrical-panel routes retain commercial intent.

Safety boundary: the plan does not authorize unqualified electrical work, fuel-system work, live synchronization, protection-setting changes, lifting, confined-space entry, hot work, or running an engine in an unsafe enclosure. Exact ratings, cable sizes, protection settings, ventilation, exhaust, tank, emission, noise, and inspection requirements must come from current primary rules, manufacturer data, project calculations, and qualified professionals.

Main risks:

- location templates can act as doorway pages when the only change is a place name;
- unsafe DIY generator, AVR, synchronization, wiring, repair, and fuel guidance;
- confusion between standby, prime, continuous, kW, kVA, power factor, phase, voltage, and frequency;
- universal sizing rules that ignore motor starting, nonlinear loads, derating, load steps, redundancy, and future growth;
- fuel/exhaust/ventilation arrangements that create fire, spill, carbon-monoxide, heat, or backpressure hazards;
- brand, stock, low-price, nationwide service, warranty, and emissions claims without dated evidence;
- archiving and video sitemaps being mistaken for topical coverage.

## Consolidation plan

1. Export live GSC/query/backlink/lead evidence before changing any of the 4,393 location URLs.
2. Establish one commercial owner for each of the nine families; retain local pages only when service operations, inventory, logistics, rules, case evidence, and contact handling are substantively local.
3. Cluster the 62 legacy articles against the 108 briefs. Keep the strongest historical URL for each intent and merge useful material rather than publishing another duplicate.
4. Remove unsafe DIY generator-building, live AVR, wiring, synchronization, and repair instructions or rewrite them as risk-aware conceptual pages with stop conditions.
5. Reconcile sitemap sources so only canonical indexable URLs appear; archives, cache paths, duplicates, and invalid video entries should not inflate coverage.
6. Keep supported brand pages only when product/support evidence is current.

- GEN-02 links to use-case requirements in GEN-16 and procurement in GEN-18.
- GEN-03 through GEN-10 form the design/system layer.
- GEN-11 provides the handoff from installation to GEN-12 operation and GEN-13 maintenance.
- GEN-13 links symptoms to GEN-14; GEN-14 links intervention decisions to GEN-15.
- GEN-17 modifies fuel, exhaust, noise, and procurement decisions with current environmental evidence.
- Commercial CTAs appear only after scope/requirement pages, not as substitutes for technical answers.
- Every article links to its parent and at least two contextually useful sibling/lifecycle pages.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current official regulation status and applicability on the publication date.
- Use manufacturer manuals for the exact model, not generic values copied between units.
- Require qualified electrical/mechanical/fire/environmental review for safety- or compliance-critical claims.
- Never invent ratings, capacity, cable/protection settings, tank dimensions, ventilation area, exhaust diameter/backpressure, acoustic performance, fuel consumption, emission results, service coverage, price, stock, warranty, certification, or case outcomes.
- Clearly label calculations, assumptions, units, derating, exclusions, and stop conditions.
- Original field photos, nameplates, commissioning traces, oil analysis, load-bank results, and anonymized defect records are preferred evidence.
- AI diagrams may explain a system but do not prove installed condition or compliance.
- Date-stamp regulation, price, fuel, emission, brand, and technology pages.
